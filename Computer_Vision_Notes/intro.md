applications of computer vision:
- Searching through videos
	- This is done by tagging each frame with the name of the objects in it. This is useful when you are trying to search through hours of security footage for a robbery, you could simply search for the tag of a human or what ever they were wearing depending on how the videos were tagged. 
- Converting images to different versions
	- Horses to zebras, nature scene from summer to winter
	- converting movement from a video to a still image, converting dance moves from a video to a picture of a human.
